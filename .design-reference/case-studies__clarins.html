Clarins Australia achieved higher product visibility, increased qualified traffic, and improved conversion rates through a combined SEO and Google Ads strategy — resulting in significant growth in online sales and annual revenue.

Increased Website Traffic: By optimising product pages for high-traffic keywords like "best anti-aging cream" and "natural skincare products," Clarins Australia saw a notable increase in organic traffic. Implementing effective e-commerce SEO strategies enhanced product discoverability, driving more qualified visitors to the site. This boost in traffic resulted in more purchases and a stronger online presence.

Improved ROI: A targeted Google Ads campaign, combined with ongoing SEO efforts, delivered an impressive return on investment. By running tailored search ads for Clarins' best-selling products and seasonal promotions, we attracted high-intent customers, which significantly lowered the cost-per-acquisition. The balance between paid ads and organic growth strategies increased overall profitability.

Revenue Growth Over the course of a year, e-commerce conversion rates improved through site optimisations, enhanced user experience, and effective product recommendations. This, paired with the increased traffic from SEO and paid ads, resulted in a substantial increase in online sales, boosting overall revenue and solidifying Clarins' position in the competitive skincare market
